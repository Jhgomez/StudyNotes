## CNC vs PLC
* **Purpose**: CNC machines are specialized for precision machining tasks, while PLCs are designed for broader industrial automation and control of processes.

* **Programming**: CNC machines use G-code for programming, focusing on tool movements and machining operations. In contrast, PLCs typically use Ladder Logic or other programming languages to manage system operations and respond to inputs. 

* **Applications**: CNC is primarily used in manufacturing for creating parts, whereas PLCs are used for controlling entire systems and processes in various industries. 


* **Complexity**: CNC machines require a good understanding of machining processes and CAD/CAM software, while PLCs are often easier to program for those familiar with electrical systems. 


In summary, while both CNC and PLC machines play crucial roles in industrial automation, they serve distinct functions and are tailored for different applications. Understanding these differences can help in selecting the right system for specific manufacturing needs.
